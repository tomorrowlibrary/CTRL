liquidity gaps, with sudden blips of no participation, you will be the only one posting buys or sells, you can set them as you desire 
whale orders, with sudden blips of extreme participation, you will be able to post your desired buy or sell, your order will be matched by someone buying out the entire book.

# statistical arbirage

1 pairs trading Z score diversions. The simple idea is that say Pepsi and Cola are correlated, Pepsi's price moves up a little, Cola's moves down a little, you hedge between the two and revert to 0 or reverse positions, when the correlation corrects.
2 pairs trading lead lag jumps. The idea is that a group of assets follow a leader, when a jump occurs in that leader, you know the direction of the rest within a time delay. You buy or sell. to match the jump.
3 pairs trading lead lag follow. The idea is simply the longer time horizon version of the jump. Instead of a sudden event, the components or followers are behind by a bit and if the expected move is profitable you buy or sell the components 
4 basket arbitrage, If a basket, etf or grouping is strongly correlated, but one of the components is mismatched in pricing, you buy it or sell it to match the fact it will go back to its correlation price
5 exchange arbitrage, if a price is mismatched across exchanges, you buy/sell to the corresponding correction
6 latency arbitrage, if a price should be corrected for new information published, but hasn't yet, you buy/sell to match.
